this project. These original generated portions are dedicated to CC0 1.0.

Original license and the three selected source OGGs are retained in
`tool/audio_sources/kenney/`. No service is contacted during gameplay.

The metal effect contributes to coin transients; the glass effect to
star break/contact; the wood effect to soft collisions and subtle rolling grains. Absorption, shared
ambient loops, musical tones and upgrade/other cues use original synthesis.
